[GitCMS](https://gitcms.dev/) is a Git-based CMS for code-first Astro sites. Your team, clients, and AI agents can edit, review, and publish Markdown and MDX content in the browser, while the content stays in your GitHub repository.

- [Live demo: an Astro repo in GitCMS](https://gitcms.dev/withastro/astro)
