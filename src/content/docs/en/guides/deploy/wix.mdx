[Wix-managed headless](https://dev.wix.com/docs/go-headless/wix-managed-headless/about-wix-managed-headless) hosts Astro sites on Wix's infrastructure and connects them to Wix business services such as bookings, stores, CMS collections and members, with hosting, environment variables and domains managed from the Wix dashboard.

- [Official Wix guide to connecting an existing Astro project to Wix-managed headless](https://dev.wix.com/docs/go-headless/wix-managed-headless/full-integration-astro/get-started/connect-an-existing-astro-project)
